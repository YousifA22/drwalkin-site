This policy explains how the Dr. Walk-In app (“the app”) handles your personal and health information. The app is operated for Dr. Walk-In, a medical clinic with locations at 1195 Hunt Club Road and 2500 St. Laurent Blvd. in Ottawa, Ontario (“the clinic”, “we”, “us”). The clinic is a health information custodian under Ontario’s Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004 (PHIPA) and handles your information as that law requires.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your name, title and gender
  • Your date of birth
  • Your email address (used to sign in) and phone number
  • Your home address
  • Your health coverage: your Ontario health card number, version code and expiry date, or your private insurer and policy number
  • A password, which we keep only as a salted one-way hash (PBKDF2). No one at the clinic can see it.

When you book a visit, we collect the clinic, date and time you choose and the reason for your visit that you type. If you allow notifications, we collect a push notification token for your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and let you sign in
  • To book, change and cancel your appointments
  • To find your existing record in the clinic’s electronic medical record (EMR), or create one. An existing record is connected to your account only when your health card number, name and date of birth all match it.
  • To keep your clinic record up to date when you change your details in the app. Only the details you change are sent.
  • To send appointment confirmations and reminders by text message and push notification, and password reset codes by text message
  • To confirm your coverage for billing

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing.

3. Who we share it with

We share your information only with the service providers that run the app, and only so they can provide that service:

  • Oscar Pro: the clinic’s electronic medical record system, where your patient record and appointments are kept
  • Supabase: stores your app account and appointment records
  • Cloudflare: runs the app’s server
  • Twilio: sends text messages
  • Expo, Apple and Google: deliver push notifications

Some of these providers may store or process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country. We may also disclose information where the law requires or permits it.

4. How we protect it

  • All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes.
  • Viewing or changing your personal information in the app requires your password.
  • Your phone keeps only your sign-in session, in its secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). Your details are kept on our servers, not on your phone.
  • Access to our systems is limited to the people who need it to run the clinic and the app.

5. Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete Account. This immediately removes your app account and your appointment history from the app’s database. See Delete your account for details.

Deleting your app account does not delete your medical record at the clinic. Ontario law requires physicians to keep medical records for a set period (generally at least 10 years), so your clinic record, including appointments booked through the app, is kept for that time.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details at any time in the app, under Account → Personal Information.
  • Turn off appointment reminders under Account → Notifications, or turn off notifications in your phone’s settings.
  • Ask the clinic for a copy of your health record, or ask us to correct it.
  • If you have a concern about how we handle your information, please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario at www.ipc.on.ca.

7. Children

The app is for people 16 and older. Parents or guardians booking for a younger child should contact the clinic.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app about important changes.
