Delete it in the app

If you have upcoming visits, cancel them first (or call the clinic). Deleting your account does not cancel appointments already booked with the clinic.

  1. Open the Dr. Walk-In app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Can’t sign in?

Call the clinic at (613) 260-3030 or ask at the front desk, and we will delete your account for you once we have confirmed who you are.

What is deleted

  • Your app account: your sign-in email and password, name, date of birth, phone number, address and health coverage details
  • Your appointment history in the app
  • Your device’s notification token

What the clinic keeps

Your medical record at the clinic is not deleted. Ontario law requires physicians to keep medical records for a set period (generally at least 10 years), so your clinic record, including visits booked through the app, is kept for that time. You can still ask the clinic for a copy of it.
