Common questions

How do I book a visit?

Create an account, open the Book tab, choose a clinic, a day and an open time, and tell us briefly why you're coming in. You'll get a confirmation by text, and a reminder the day before.

I've been to the clinic before. Will the app find my record?

Yes. When you sign up with your Ontario health card, the app connects your account to your existing clinic record, but only if your health card number, name and date of birth all match what the clinic has on file. If they don't match, the app won't connect them. Check that your details match your health card exactly, or call the clinic.

How do I change or cancel an appointment?

Open the appointment in the app. Changes and cancellations can be made in the app up to 24 hours before your visit. A visit that is missed, or cancelled with less than 24 hours’ notice, counts as a no-show, and the clinic charges a $50 fee, paid before your next appointment.

How do I update my details?

Go to Account → Personal Information and enter your password. Tap the detail you want to change, then press Save. Changes are sent to your clinic record too.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email. We'll text a reset code to the phone number on your account.

How do I turn off reminders?

Go to Account → Notifications and switch off Appointment Reminder. You can also turn off notifications for the app in your phone's settings.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account → Delete Account. See Delete your account for what is removed and what the clinic must keep.
